<b>RecordYou-pro is a privacy-first voice and screen recorder that never loses a recording — even if the app crashes.</b>

It is a maintained fork of the unmaintained "Record You" app: same privacy guarantees, plus the reliability and convenience features below.

<b>Your recording survives a crash</b>
Lossless recordings are kept as a valid WAV file at every moment (the header is re-sealed every few seconds), so a crash leaves a playable file instead of an unreadable raw dump. The next time you open the app it offers to recover the interrupted recording — save it, delete it or ignore it.

<b>Saving is instant and never blocks you</b>
Saving just seals the header and moves the file, so long recordings stop instantly and are written in the background. A small banner shows progress and the result, and you can start the next recording right away.

<b>Behaviour switches</b>
<br>• Tap anywhere to record (the record button is hidden)
<br>• Keep recording when the app is swiped away
<br>• Hide the app from background tasks
<br>• Go to the desktop right after a recording starts
<br>• Start recording automatically when the app opens

<b>Everything the original did</b>
<br>• Lossless WAV recording, plus M4A, AAC, 3GP and OPUS
<br>• Screen recording (H.264, H.265, VP8, VP9) with an annotation overlay
<br>• Pause and resume, live waveform, timer, built-in player and lossless trimmer
<br>• Choose any output folder (SAF), custom file naming patterns
<br>• Material Design 3 with dynamic colour and dark theme

<b>Privacy</b>
No ads, no trackers, and the app declares no INTERNET permission at all — nothing can leave your device.

<b>Note</b>
Removing the app from the recent-tasks list stops the recording unless you enable the matching switch; a few aggressive vendor task killers may stop recordings anyway.
